How it works

Three steps, then it runs every billing cycle.

Dunwell is a deterministic policy engine wrapped around a grounded drafting step. You keep approval control until you decide otherwise.

01

Connect your ledger

Point Dunwell at your invoices — CSV today, QuickBooks and Xero next. It reads amounts, due dates, contact details, and payment status.

02

Review the drafts

Every late invoice gets an escalating draft — courteous, firm, or final. Each one is grounded in the invoice record and ready for one-click approval.

03

It reconciles and stops

Payment lands? The sequence halts. Dispute, opt-out, or cooldown window? It holds and escalates to a human instead of pushing.

Cited or refused

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will it email my clients without me seeing it first?

Not unless you tell it to. Early access runs in approve-first mode: every draft waits in your queue until you approve it. Auto-send can be enabled per account once you trust the tone.

Does the AI ever make up invoice details?

No. The drafting step is constrained to values present on the invoice record — number, amount, due date, days overdue. If a required fact is missing, Dunwell refuses to draft and holds the invoice for you instead.

What happens when a client disputes an invoice?

Disputed invoices are pulled out of the sequence immediately and escalated to a human with the dispute note attached. The same applies to opt-outs, missing contacts, and invoices past the automation age limit.

Which accounting tools does it connect to?

Today Dunwell reads CSV ledgers and ships with a full demo ledger. QuickBooks and Xero connectors are next on the roadmap and included in the Growth plan.

How is this different from my bookkeeper's reminder emails?

A reminder tool sends a template. Dunwell decides — per invoice — whether a reminder should go out at all, how firm it should be, whether it must wait, and when to stop, then shows you exactly why.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Early access is month-to-month with no contract, and the 50% lifetime discount stays as long as your subscription remains active.
