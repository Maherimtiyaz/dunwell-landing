Privacy Policy

Short version: Dunwell uses your invoice data to decide which customers to chase and to write the follow-ups you approve. It does not sell your data, and it does not train models on your ledger.

What we collect

  • Waitlist email. If you join the waitlist we store your email address and a source tag (for example, "landing") so we know how you found us.
  • Account details. When you create an account we store your email address and a salted password hash. We never store or can recover your password.
  • Ledger data you import. The invoices and customer records you upload — invoice numbers, amounts, due dates, statuses, customer names, and the email address a reminder would go to.
  • Nudge history. A record of every follow-up you approve and send: which invoice, what tone, to whom, and when. This is what stops us from chasing someone twice in the same week.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data, or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not train AI models on your ledger. Drafts are generated from the facts on your own invoice records.
  • We do not send anything to your customers without you approving it first. There is no automatic sending.

How your data is stored

During early access the service runs on a single host with a local SQLite database. Data is stored in the country where the service is hosted. We do not run a separate analytics pipeline over your ledger.

Invoices are only ever read from the accounting system you connect. We do not modify, void, or pay anything on your behalf.

Email we send

If you enable email delivery, follow-ups you approve are sent through our mail provider using your company name and a reply-to address you control — replies go to you, not to us. Recipients can ask to be skipped, and marking a customer as opted out stops all further contact.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove your ledger, nudge history, and account details. Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them.

Your rights

  • Ask what we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Have your data deleted.
  • Opt out of non-essential email at any time.

Cookies

The site sets one cookie, and only after you create an account: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It contains no tracking identifier and is not used across other sites.

Changes

If this policy changes materially — for example, if we add a subprocessor for hosting or email — we will note it here and, for waitlist subscribers, tell you by email.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or corrections: privacy@dunwell.app.
